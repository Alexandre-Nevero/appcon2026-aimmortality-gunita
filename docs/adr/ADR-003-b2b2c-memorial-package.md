# ADR-003 — Business model hypothesis: free family archive, paid Memorial package via funeral partners

- **Date:** 2026-09-23
- **Status:** Accepted (as the hypothesis to pitch; unvalidated)
- **Owners / agents:** Alex (team lead)
- **Related:** `docs/brd.md`; PRD Open questions (sustainability story)

### Context
The judging criteria give 10 points to Maintainability & Sustainability ("Can this project grow
and actually last?"). The finalized product decision contains no business model. The TALA tab
proposed freemium plus premium plans and funeral-home partnerships; the UGAT tab proposed a
one-time family package and B2B2C distribution.

### Why now
The BRD and pitch need one clear sustainability story.

### Options considered
1. **Free family archive + paid Memorial package sold through funeral homes and memorial parks.**
   Pros: the paid moment (the wake) already has a buyer and an existing funeral-services purchase;
   partners provide distribution. Cons: depends on partner deals; unvalidated.
2. **One-time family preservation package.** Pros: simple. Cons: the family must decide to pay
   before any loss, which the UGAT tab lists as a risk ("people care after death, not before").
3. **Freemium with premium family plan.** Cons: recurring payment for access to one's own
   memories may feel wrong; hard to justify for a grief product.

### Decision
Pitch option 1. The During archive stays free for families. Memorial Mode outputs (published recap,
QR, visitor wall, moderation) form the paid Memorial package, sold directly or through funeral
homes and memorial parks. Price is unknown until tested.

### Why this option
It keeps the archive free, so families aren't charged for access to their own memories.
Monetization sits at a moment when a purchase already happens, through a channel that already
reaches families.

### Overrides
- **Prior ADRs:** none
- **Doc / plan truth:** resolves the PRD open question "Sustainability story" for the pitch.
- **Out of scope:** No payments are built in the MVP.

### Consequences
- **Easier:** a clear sustainability slide.
- **Harder / owed:** willingness-to-pay and partner interest are unvalidated.
- **Follow-up:** validation steps in `docs/brd.md`.
